# Descaler sachet stock

The noter's six-month estimate is 5 sachets, and both inputs behind it are bets.

- Demand: the user's 2 kg of beans a month at the 18 g dose is about 111 shots a month, about 4.4 descales at 150 shots in six months. [[notes/sachet-demand-six-months]] (bet)
- Margin: shots since the last descale are unrecorded, so one extra sachet covers the carry-over. [[notes/last-descale-shot-count-unknown]] (bet)

The usage rate is the weak point: it treats the household's beans as all shots, and the two-doubles-a-day bet gives a different count (the answer is still 5). [[_user/notes/beans-2kg-per-month]], [[_user/notes/two-double-shots-a-day]]. Both bets close when the user gives shots per day and shots since the last descale, counted from the descale, not the counter ([[_user/notes/feedback_shot_counting]]).
